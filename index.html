Our Story

At Auralis, we believe that bathing is more than a routine — it's a moment of personal sanctuary. Born from a desire to bring luxury and tranquility into everyday life, we are inspired by the ethereal beauty of auroras and natural harmony of the universe. Our name reflects this vision — like an aurora, Auralis radiates a gentle brilliance that transforms the ordinary into the extraordinary. Every product is designed to nourish the body, calm the mind and elevate the senses, turning simple moments into indulgent rituals. Welcome to Auralis — Illuminate Your Ritual

Vision

To be India’s most trusted bath and home solutions brand, blending design, technology and care to elevate everyday living.
